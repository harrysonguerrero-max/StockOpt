    Raw data

    The dataset tables untranslated and unrounded, with the definition of every column — and, above them, how the catalogue reads on the three dimensions that decide where control goes.

    Criticality · Value · Rotation

    Criticality answers what happens if the part is missing, value answers how much capital it moves, and rotation answers how often it moves. None of the three is enough on its own, and that is exactly why they are crossed.

    Where the value is

    Cumulative share of annual consumption value, parts ordered from most to least valuable. The question is not what each part is worth but how few parts it takes to reach 80% of the spend. Colour is criticality.

    Value against rotation

    Each bubble is a part. Right means it moves often, up means it moves money. Colour is criticality — the one dimension neither axis can derive, because it is a maintenance judgement and not a measurement.
